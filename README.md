## Summary of the dataset

My dataset looks at different categaories and stats relating to various types of car brands and their models. <br>
Some of these stats include:
- Kilometers driven
- Fuel type
- Milage

### Questions and Hypothesis
I will aim to answer the following five questions:

1. What is the relationship between the type of engine and the power it produces?
    - There is a linear relationship between engine type and the power produced by the car. As the engine type goes up the power goes up.
2. Does the price of the car increase the power from the engine?
    - No the price of the car doesn't increase the power from the engine because some expensive brands will have some slower cars.
3. Does the fuel type effect the milage on the car?
    - Deisel cars will have a higher milage then petrol and other fuel type cars.
4. Have cars being produced become more automatic over time?
    - The amount of automatic cars has increase as time as gone on and the amount of manual cars being produced is going down every year.
5. How much does the brand of car affect the price and how much variety in price does each brand have?
    - The brand of car will massivly change the price and each brand will have a large distribution of prices.
